About

What I Do

I am a full-stack software developer based in Nairobi. I design and build custom web systems, company websites and mobile applications, and I run them in production afterwards — hosting, domains, backups, monitoring and support.

Most of my work is for the Kenyan market, so payments, connectivity and cost matter as much as the code. I have built and maintain live systems that handle real customer money through M-Pesa daily, and I understand what it takes to keep a platform running reliably on local infrastructure rather than just shipping a site and disappearing.

I work directly with clients, which means one point of contact, no account managers, and honest advice about what a project actually needs.
